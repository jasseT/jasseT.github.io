Who am I?

Well isn’t that the question.

Professionally speaking:

  • Multitalented professional, generalist, blue sky thinker.
  • MSc, Information studies and interactive media;
    BSc, Human-computer interaction.
  • Interested in all things concerning HCI and UX, users and usability testing.
  • Last ten years as a freelancer in the translation and subtitling business.
  • Don’t code much, but fiddle about with AI sometimes.

Then for some other questions.

  • What's the point of this site? I'm not sure, really.
  • Am I looking to get hired? Technically, yes.
  • What can I do? Many things. My two main areas of interest are IT and language.

Unprofessionally speaking, I write, read and translate, and spend most of my time finding things out about various topics. I like having a good time and not restricting myself, life is way too short to spend on things one doesn't like.

When thinking about the tone of this text, I wondered should it be more serious, or "professional," as they say. But that would be somewhat dishonest.

Creativity and problem solving stem from a free flow of thought, which shouldn’t be stifled with overly restrictive rules. If I need to write a serious text or an academic article, sure I will do it, and do it well, without fooling around. But that is the job, not me, and after all, the question this chapter is trying to answer is “who am I”. Below is me thinking about that.
