Who am I?

Well isn’t that the question.

Professionally speaking:

  • Multitalent, generalist, blue sky thinker.
  • MSc, Information studies and interactive media;
    BSc, Human-computer interaction.
  • Past 13 years as a freelancer in the translation and subtitling business.
  • Interested in all things concerning linguistics, HCI and UX, users and usability testing.
  • Don’t code much, but fiddle about with AI sometimes.

Then for some other questions.

  • What's the point of this site?
    I'm not sure, really.
  • Am I looking to get hired?
    Technically, yes.
  • What can I do?
    Many things. My two main areas of interest are IT and language.

Unprofessionally speaking, I write, read and fiddle with tech most of the time. I usually have something going on, be it writing, computing or fixing broken things; or learning something new and interesting. Life is way too short to spend on things one doesn't like.

Creativity and problem solving stem from a free flow of thought, which shouldn’t be stifled with overly restrictive rules. Having said that, if I need to write a serious text or an academic article, sure I will do it, and do it well, without fooling around. But that is the job, not me, and after all, the question this chapter is trying to answer is "who am I." Below is me thinking about that.
